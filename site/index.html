VoltForge

Battery control for Android phones without root. It trims brightness, sync, background apps and more, on a schedule you set.

Download APK

What it does

  • Shows drain rate, temperature and battery health
  • One-tap Saver and Extreme profiles, with a full restore
  • Cleans background apps while the screen is off
  • Switches to Extreme at your low-battery level

Install

  • Tap Download APK, then open the file
  • Allow installs from this browser when asked
  • Open VoltForge and grant the two power-ups at the top
